WHOIS / DOMAIN INTELLIGENCE REPORT
1. Target Information

Domain: example.com
Date Investigated: 23 September 2026
Investigation Type: Passive Domain Intelligence
Authorization: Educational / documentation exercise

2. Registration Information

Registrar / Registration Authority: Internet Assigned Numbers Authority (IANA)
Creation Date: 1 January 1992
Domain Age: Approximately 34 years
Nameserver Information: Registration information for the example domain is maintained by IANA.
Privacy Protection: Not applicable in the normal commercial-domain sense because example.com is an IANA-reserved example domain.
Domain Status: Reserved for documentation/example use.

IANA states that example.com and other example domains are maintained for documentation purposes and are not available for normal registration or transfer.

3. Important Observations
Finding 1 — Very old creation date

The domain is recorded as having been created on 1 January 1992.

Interpretation:
The domain is not a newly registered domain. However, because it is an IANA-reserved example domain, its age should not be compared directly with the age of an ordinary commercial domain.

Finding 2 — IANA maintains the domain

The WHOIS information identifies the organization as Internet Assigned Numbers Authority (IANA).

Interpretation:
This is consistent with IANA's documentation that example.com is reserved for examples and documentation.

Finding 3 — The domain is reserved

IANA explicitly states that example.com is maintained for documentation purposes and is not available for registration or transfer.

Interpretation:
This is not a normal domain-registration case. It should therefore be treated as a safe educational example rather than a real-world suspicious-domain investigation.

4. Potential Security Significance

There is no basis from the registration information alone to classify example.com as malicious.

The main lesson from this investigation is how domain-registration information can provide context.

For a real suspicious domain, useful indicators might include:

Very recent registration
Suspicious domain naming
Registration information that changes unexpectedly
Infrastructure associated with known malicious activity
Other threat-intelligence indicators

However:

A newly registered domain does not automatically mean that the domain is malicious.

Additional evidence would always be required.

5. Additional Evidence Required for a Real Investigation

If investigating an actual suspicious domain, an analyst could correlate registration information with:

DNS records
Website content
URL reputation
Threat-intelligence reports
Certificate information
Email context
Domain similarity to legitimate organizations
Known malicious infrastructure

The goal is to combine multiple observations rather than making a decision from one indicator.

6. Limitations
example.com is an IANA-reserved example domain rather than a normal commercial domain.
Therefore, this investigation does not demonstrate how a real suspicious domain's ownership or privacy-protected registration information would appear.
WHOIS/registration information alone cannot establish whether a domain is malicious.
Registration information may be limited or privacy-protected for ordinary domains.
7. Final Conclusion

The investigation shows that domain-registration data can provide useful contextual information such as creation date and registration authority.

For example.com, the information is consistent with its special purpose as an IANA-reserved documentation domain.
