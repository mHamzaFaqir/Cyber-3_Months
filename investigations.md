# Investigation Notes

## Digital Footprints

When investigating a public digital footprint, don't focus only on what is visible now.

Useful questions include:

- What was published previously?
- What changed?
- What was removed?
- What accounts or platforms are connected?
- Where does the same person practice or document cybersecurity?

### Reminder

A current page is only one point in time.

History can provide context that the current version does not.

























































## Observation

The most useful evidence is not always in the final version.

Compare the changes carefully.
